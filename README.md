## Mistakes that void your cash back

Most "my cash back never showed up" stories trace back to one of these:

- **Shopping in Safari**, or clicking through on mobile and getting bounced into the Walmart app — the tracking cookie dies in the handoff. On a phone, finish the checkout in the mobile browser.
- **A full cart at click-through.** Clear your Walmart cart before clicking the portal link; a pre-loaded cart is a leading cause of untracked orders.
- **Ad blockers and coupon extensions** interfering with the tracking click. Disable them for the purchase session.
- **Grabbing a coupon code from a third-party site mid-checkout.** That can override your TopCashback attribution. Stick to codes shown on the TopCashback page itself.
- **Mixing groceries into the same order.** Online grocery purchases earn nothing and, per Walmart's affiliate terms, can jeopardize the cash back on the retail items in the same cart. Keep the TV order separate from the weekly grocery run.
- **Clicking through to Walmart earlier in the day**, then buying later from a direct visit. The last qualifying click-through wins.
- **Returning the TV.** The cash back reverses with the refund.
